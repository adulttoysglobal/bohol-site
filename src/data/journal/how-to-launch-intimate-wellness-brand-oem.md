## 1. Private Label (White Label) vs. Custom Proprietary Tooling

For early-stage startups with limited capital ($5,000 to $15,000), white labeling existing BOHOL catalog designs (such as [Mengqu](/products/mengqu/) or [Summer Rabbit](/products/summer-rabbit/)) is the fastest route to market. You can customize Pantone body colors, silk-screen logos, custom gift boxes, and instruction manuals in as little as 25 days. For established brands raising venture capital, developing a custom liquid silicone injection mold (costing $12,000–$25,000) provides exclusive patented geometry that competitors cannot replicate.

## 2. The 5-Phase Manufacturing Roadmap

• **Phase 1: Industrial Design & DFM (Days 1–7):** CAD 3D modeling, wall thickness optimization, and parting line placement.<br>• **Phase 2: Functional 3D Prototyping (Days 8–12):** SLA/SLS resin printing to test hand ergonomics and internal motor mounting.<br>• **Phase 3: Precision Steel Tooling (Days 13–35):** 5-axis CNC mold cutting and mirror spark EDM polishing.<br>• **Phase 4: T1 Pilot Samples & Regulatory Testing (Days 36–42):** First article inspection, RoHS testing, drop tests, and IPX7 verification.<br>• **Phase 5: Mass Cleanroom Production & Export (Days 43–60):** Automated injection molding, packaging assembly, and sea/air logistics.
