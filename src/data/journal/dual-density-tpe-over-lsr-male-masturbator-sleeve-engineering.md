## 1. The Structural Dilemma of Automated Male Strokers

Automated linear strokers subject inner sleeves to relentless mechanical shear forces. If a sleeve is made entirely of ultra-soft elastomer (Shore 00-05), the motor's clamping carriage will tear the material during high-speed 350 RPM reciprocating cycles. Conversely, if the sleeve is firm enough to withstand mechanical drive, it lacks the anatomical compliance required for premium sensory stimulation.

BOHOL resolves this conflict through proprietary **two-shot dual-density overmolding**:
1. An outer rigid skeleton molded from Shore 50A liquid silicone rubber provides positive mechanical indexing and resists drive-rod shearing.
2. An inner sensory sleeve molded from ultra-soft Shore 00-05 elastomer conforms dynamically to anatomical contours without tearing.

## 2. Molecular Bonding & Delamination Prevention

Because platinum-cured LSR and styrene-block TPE possess divergent thermal expansion coefficients, thermal delamination has historically plagued multi-layer sleeves.

BOHOL utilizes plasma surface functionalization and mechanical undercut interlocks to establish molecular adhesion across the bonding boundary, withstanding over 500 wash-and-disinfect cycles without layer separation.
