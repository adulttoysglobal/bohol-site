## 1. Cellular Independence: Zero Guest Wi-Fi Headaches

Hotel IT directors routinely reject third-party IoT hardware that requires connecting to guest Wi-Fi networks due to cybersecurity compliance (PCI-DSS) and captive-portal login friction. BOHOL kiosks feature embedded global multi-carrier eSIMs. Each machine connects autonomously over encrypted 4G/NB-IoT cellular towers, ensuring 99.98% uptime with zero burden on hotel IT infrastructure.

## 2. Housekeeping Efficiency: The Smart Restock Route

Housekeeping carts are pre-loaded with exact merchandise counts, cutting restocking time to under 45 seconds per room.
