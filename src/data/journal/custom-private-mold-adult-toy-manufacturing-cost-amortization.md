## 1. The Anatomy of Private Mold Tooling: Preventing Tactile Defects

In the premium sexual wellness category, industrial design and tactile perfection dictate consumer retail success. When an adult novelty arrives with visible parting-line ridges (flash), consumers perceive it as cheap, unhygienic, or physically uncomfortable.

Because liquid silicone rubber (LSR) exhibits water-like viscosity under 180°C injection pressures, the slightest micro-deflection in mold plates causes silicone to seep across parting lines. Achieving a truly seamless, flash-free finish requires high-rigidity hardened steel molds, precision optical alignment, and vacuum-assisted cavity degassing prior to shot injection.
