## What Biological and Chemical Contaminants Threaten Open-Workshop Silicone Production?

Liquid silicone rubber acts as an electrostatically active polymer during the vulcanization phase. In standard workshops, airborne cutting oil from adjacent metal lathes, carbon soot from machinery, and human skin dander adhere to the hot molds. These foreign contaminants degrade the molecular cross-linking of platinum silicone, creating soft spots that leach mineral oils, attract lint, and harbor Candida and Staphylococcus bacteria after water contact.
