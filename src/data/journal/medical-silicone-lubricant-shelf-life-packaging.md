## What Chemical Compounds Differentiate Premium Medical Silicone from Industrial Grade?

Industrial silicone oils often contain low-viscosity cyclic siloxanes (D4, D5, D6), which are known environmental toxins and skin irritants banned under EU REACH Annex XVII. In contrast, BOHOL's medical-grade formula uses exclusively long-chain linear Polydimethylsiloxanes (PDMS) comprising 100 cSt Dimethicone blended with high-viscosity Dimethiconol. This creates an ultra-slippery, non-absorbing protective lipid film that never leaves a greasy or sticky residue.

## Why Are Nitrogen Flushing and Induction Foil Seals Essential for Ocean Export?

During ocean container transit through tropical zones, shipping containers experience internal temperatures exceeding 55°C. Atmospheric oxygen trapped in bottle headspaces can oxidize trace packaging additives, while air pressure swings cause bottle bulging and leakage. BOHOL's automated bottling line purges the bottle neck with inert 99.99% pure nitrogen gas immediately before high-frequency induction sealing, locking in freshness for 5 full years.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can silicone lubricants be washed off with plain water?
Because pure silicone is completely hydrophobic and insoluble in water, plain water will not rinse it off. It washes away cleanly with mild unscented hand soap or body wash.

### Is silicone lubricant safe for anal play and shower/bath use?
Yes. Silicone lubricant is particularly suited for bath, hot tub, and shower use because water does not dissolve or wash it away, maintaining effortless glide in aquatic environments.
