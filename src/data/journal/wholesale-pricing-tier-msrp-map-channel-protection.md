## What Is the Classic 4-Tier Pricing Multiplier Formula in Adult Retail?

Healthy consumer wellness economics follow an engineered price multiplier: <br/>• **Tier 1: Factory Landed COGS** = $12.00 (Manufacturing cost from BOHOL).<br/>• **Tier 2: Master Distributor Wholesale** = $22.00 (Volume orders > 1,000 units; 45% distributor gross margin).<br/>• **Tier 3: Boutique Retail Wholesale (Wholesale)** = $32.00 (Small orders 50-100 units to retail shops).<br/>• **Tier 4: Consumer MSRP / MAP** = $79.00 (Final retail price; keystone 2.47x markup for retailers).<br/>This structure ensures that every intermediary in the supply chain earns sustainable profit, incentivizing sales reps to actively promote your brand.

## How Does a Strict MAP Policy Prevent Rogue Amazon Resellers from Destroying Margins?

Under US antitrust law (Leegin precedent), manufacturers can legally enforce a unilateral Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) policy. The policy stipulates that any retailer or online seller who advertises a product below the designated MAP (e.g. $79.00) will have their wholesale supply cut off immediately.
