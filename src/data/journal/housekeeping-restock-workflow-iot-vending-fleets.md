## How Does Cloud Telemetry Transform the Morning Housekeeping Routine?

At 07:00 AM each morning, our BOHOL Cloud Fleet portal automatically aggregates live inventory across all hotel rooms. Instead of inspecting 200 rooms, the report indicates that only 14 rooms had transactions overnight (e.g. Room 402 purchased 1 condom pack; Room 618 purchased 1 lubricant). Housekeeping attendants place exactly 14 pre-boxed units onto their supply caddy. Staff enter only the 14 specified rooms during routine cleaning, saving hours of unnecessary labor.

## What Is the Physical Restocking Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)?

1. Attendant taps the authorized RFID Master Card against the terminal's contactless scanner. The solenoid locks release with an audible click.<br/>2. The front door swings open on heavy-duty internal hinges.<br/>3. Staff slide the designated merchandise into the matching spiral slot without requiring tools or screws.<br/>4. The door is pushed closed; optical sensors immediately scan the tray and update cloud inventory to '100% Full' within 5 seconds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can the hotel set custom permission levels for housekeeping vs hotel managers?
Yes. Master RFID cards can be configured for 'Restock Only' (which opens the door but displays zero sales or revenue figures), while managerial credentials provide access to financial analytics and pricing overrides.

### What happens if a housekeeping attendant accidentally loads the wrong SKU into a slot?
The cloud portal allows managers to verify slot configurations remotely, and internal barcode verification scanners can be enabled to beep if an incorrect item dimension is inserted.
