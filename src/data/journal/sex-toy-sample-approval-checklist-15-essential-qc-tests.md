## 1. Functional, Acoustic & Waterproof Tests (Checks 1–8)

Before approving any OEM or wholesale order, evaluate the hardware across eight core physical benchmarks: (1) motor resonance under manual palm load, (2) acoustic decibel rating at 30 cm, (3) 60-minute continuous waterproof submersion, (4) magnetic charging pin alignment and salt-spray resistance, (5) PCB standby drain over 14 days, (6) thermal ceiling under 43°C, (7) button actuation durability across 10,000 presses, and (8) Shore hardness consistency across the silicone sleeve.

| Inspection Stage | Typical Trader Spot-Check | BOHOL 15-Point Golden Sample Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Acoustic Noise Test** | Subjective ear check in loud office | Anechoic soundroom test at 30 cm (<38–42 dB) |
| **Waterproof Verification** | Quick tap-water rinse | -50 kPa dry vacuum decay + 60-min IPX8 immersion |
| **Battery & Charge Cycle** | Verify LED turns on | 72-hour continuous load aging + PCM overcharge cutoff |
| **Surface & Seam Finish** | Visual glance only | 20x magnification parting-line check + 70% IPA wipe test |

## 2. Material, Packaging & Compliance Verification (Checks 9–15)

The remaining seven checks eliminate customs holds and marketplace chargebacks: (9) zero odor after 40°C warm water immersion, (10) silicone stretch-and-tear rebound test, (11) Pantone color match under D65 daylight, (12) 1.2-meter retail box drop test, (13) neutral exterior shipping label check, (14) matching UN38.3/FDA/RoHS lab test serials, and (15) dual-signed Golden Sample retention.
