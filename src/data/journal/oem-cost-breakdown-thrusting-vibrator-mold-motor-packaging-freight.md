## 1. Where Every Dollar of Your Landed Unit Cost Goes

Founders often underestimate the hidden gap between ex-factory hardware cost and true warehouse-landed cost. In a modern rechargeable intimate device, roughly 40% of the BOM sits inside the electromechanical core (brushless/coreless motor, lithium-ion cell with PCM safety board, waterproof magnetic charging pins, and firmware MCU), 28% in medical-grade platinum liquid silicone and ultrasonic assembly, 17% in luxury magnetic gift packaging and manuals, and 15% in UN38.3-compliant DDP freight and customs clearance.

| Cost Component | Entry Single-Motor Silicone Vibe | Flagship Dual-Motor / App Thrusting Device |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Custom Steel Mold (One-Time)** | $4,500 – $7,500 (ABS + LSR) | $10,000 – $18,000 (Multi-action gear + LSR) |
| **Electronics (Motor + PCB + Li-Ion)** | $2.10 – $3.40 / unit | $6.20 – $11.50 / unit (BLDC + BLE 5.3) |
