## 2. Doorstep Discretion: The Plain Brown Box Standard

Even the most luxurious retail gift box must arrive discreetly at the consumer's home. BOHOL packs all wholesale shipments in unmarked, heavy-duty corrugated master cartons. The shipping label lists the sender neutrally (e.g., 'BOHOL Logistics' or client brand trade name), with custom HS code classifications (HS 8509.80 for electrical appliances) that pass customs without explicit adult labeling.
