## What Mechanical Design Features Prevent Physical Pry-Open Attacks?

Traditional vending doors have external gaps where flathead screwdrivers or crowbars can be wedged. BOHOL's wall-mounted terminals employ a double-flanged labyrinth seam: the dispensing door closes into a recessed steel channel with less than 0.8mm clearance. Furthermore, rather than relying on a single mechanical cam lock, our units utilize dual heavy-duty motorized solenoids with hardened steel locking bolts rated for over 150 kgf of tensile pry resistance.

## How Does IoT Gyroscope Sensor Telemetry Deter Vandalism?

Embedded within the terminal's 4G IoT motherboard is an automotive-grade MEMS accelerometer. If a guest strikes, shakes, or attempts to dislodge the terminal, the microcontroller records the impact acceleration (G-force). If acceleration exceeds safety thresholds, the terminal instantly pings the hotel front-desk management portal with the exact room number and timestamp, allowing security to intervene before structural damage occurs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can hotel guests steal the entire vending kiosk from the wall?
No. The machine cannot be unmounted from the wall without opening the electronic door via master RFID card or encrypted cloud command, as all mounting anchor bolts reside inside the locked steel compartment.

### What happens if a product jams in the dispensing spiral?
Our infrared beam sensor detects whether a product successfully crossed the drop chute. If no drop is detected, the motor automatically reverses and attempts a second cycle. If still blocked, the card transaction is instantly reversed with zero guest dispute.
