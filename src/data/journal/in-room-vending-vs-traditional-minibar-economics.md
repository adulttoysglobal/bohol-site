## Why Do Traditional Minibars Lose Money While Intimate Vending Generates Pure Profit?

A traditional minibar costs $15 to $25 per month per room in electrical refrigeration alone. Moreover, guests routinely replace expensive miniature vodka bottles with tap water or contest charges during morning checkout rushes. Because front-desk clerks frequently waive disputed minibar fees to preserve guest review scores, hotels lose thousands annually. In contrast, BOHOL's wall kiosks draw only 4W in standby, require no compressor cooling, and never dispense an item until the customer's credit card or Apple Pay is approved by the bank gateway.
