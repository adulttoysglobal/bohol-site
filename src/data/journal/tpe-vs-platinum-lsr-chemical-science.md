## What Is the Chemical Mechanism of Plasticizer Migration in Low-Cost TPE?

Thermoplastic Elastomers consist of a physical blend of Styrene-Ethylene-Butylene-Styrene (SEBS) polymers suspended in high volumes of paraffinic or naphthenic mineral oil (often exceeding 60% by weight). Because the mineral oil molecules are merely trapped physically rather than covalently bonded, heat, friction, and moisture cause these oils to migrate rapidly to the surface. This creates the infamous 'greasy film', distinct chemical odor, and sticky dust-attracting surface typical of budget toys.

## Why Does Platinum-Cured Liquid Silicone Provide Absolute Biological Inertness?

In platinum-cured LSR, liquid vinyl-terminated polydimethylsiloxane reacts with a cross-linking hydride functional siloxane in the presence of an elemental platinum catalyst. This creates a thermoset polymer with an irreversible, three-dimensional -Si-O-Si- backbone. The resulting elastomer contains zero plasticizers, zero phthalates, zero heavy metals, and zero volatile solvents. It is chemically inert, non-porous, hypoallergenic, and can withstand sterilization temperatures up to 200°C without melting or chemical degradation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can consumers test whether a product is made of real silicone or cheap TPE?
Real silicone feels velvety and dry, never greasy, and does not carry a petroleum chemical odor. In laboratory burn tests, silicone burns with white smoke leaving clean white silica ash, whereas TPE burns with black acrid smoke and melts into a gooey tar.

### Can medical silicone products be sanitized in boiling water?
Yes. Pure platinum liquid silicone is heat-resistant up to 200°C. Waterproof models (with motors and electronics removed, or silicone sleeves detached) can be submerged in boiling water for 3 to 5 minutes for complete clinical sterilization.
