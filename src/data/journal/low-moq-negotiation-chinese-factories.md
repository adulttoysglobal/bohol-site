## Why Do Traditional Factories Demand Massive MOQs, and How Does BOHOL Solve It?

In conventional injection molding, switching liquid silicone colors requires thoroughly purging the injection barrel, recalibrating mixing pumps, and burning through hundreds of kilograms of silicone material. BOHOL's 120+ cleanroom lines include dedicated continuous-run presses dedicated to neutral, premium tones (Midnight Obsidian, Rose Petal, Slate Grey). Because these presses run uninterrupted, we can allocate production batches as small as 50 units without incurring purge waste penalties.

## How Does Rapid Laser Personalization Enable Private Labeling for Small Batches?

Rather than demanding custom-molded silicone shells for brand names, our high-precision UV fiber laser marking workstations etch client typography, logos, and regulatory symbols directly onto finished hardware in seconds. Paired with custom slipcase sleeves fitted over neutral luxury rigid boxes, startup brands receive fully branded, retail-ready products at fraction of the capital expenditure.
