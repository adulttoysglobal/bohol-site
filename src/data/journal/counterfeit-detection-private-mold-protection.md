## How Do Rogue Workshops Copy Private Molds, and How Does Air-Gapped Tooling Prevent It?

Counterfeiting rarely originates from external industrial espionage; it almost always stems from suppliers outsourcing secondary injection molding to unvetted satellite workshops. At BOHOL's 35,000 m² campus, all 5-axis CNC milling, mirror spark EDM, and Class 100,000 liquid silicone injection are vertically integrated under one roof. When a client commissions a private mold, the steel tooling blocks (NAK80 or S136 stainless steel) remain permanently stored in our monitored, humidity-controlled mold vault, dispatched to cleanroom presses strictly upon scheduled purchase order release.

## What Legal Clauses Must Every International OEM Manufacturing Agreement Include?

To establish real deterrents, overseas brands must stipulate agreed pre-set liquidated damages in their manufacturing covenants, designated under the jurisdiction of local arbitration commissions (such as CIETAC in Shenzhen or Beijing). If any unauthorized units are detected in the marketplace, liquidated damages can be seized immediately without lengthy civil litigation over difficult-to-quantify lost profits.
