## 1. Why Constant Temperature Mimicry Matters in Adult Novelties

Cold silicone is one of the most common consumer complaints in intimate devices. Bringing the device contact surface to human internal temperature (37.5°C to 42.0°C) dramatically increases tactile comfort and vascular blood flow.

However, intimate tissue is extremely sensitive to thermal burns. Temperatures exceeding 45°C risk dermal injury. Traditional resistive nichrome wire elements create localized 'hot spots' that degrade silicone and risk user safety. ## 2. Dual-Redundant Thermistor Safety Architecture

Every heated model leaving BOHOL's cleanroom integrates two micro NTC thermistors sampling temperature at 100 Hz. If temperature exceeds 43.5°C or delta-T spikes unexpectedly, the hardware microcontroller cuts power in under 15 milliseconds.

Furthermore, PTC ceramic materials have a physical Curie point: once they reach 44°C, electrical resistance increases exponentially, making runaway overheating physically impossible.
