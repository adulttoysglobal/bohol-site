## 1. The Science of Osmolality and Mucosal Integrity

Most consumers evaluate lubricants purely on slipperiness, unaware of cellular osmolality. High concentrations of propylene glycol and glycerin create extreme osmotic pressure, literally drawing moisture out of epithelial cells and leaving mucosal walls vulnerable to micro-tears.
