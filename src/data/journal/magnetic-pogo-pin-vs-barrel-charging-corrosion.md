## What Causes Galvanic Electrolysis Corrosion in Traditional DC Jack Ports?

When a user plugs a standard DC cable into a wet pinhole port, the 5V charging current passes through water containing dissolved bath salts and soap ions. This triggers rapid electrochemical electrolysis: the positive anode copper pin rapidly oxidizes and dissolves, producing copper hydroxycarbonate (the characteristic green crust). Within 3 to 5 charging cycles, the pin dissolves, breaking electrical continuity permanently.

## How Do Flush Gold-Plated Pogo-Pins Maintain Clean Surface Electrical Contact?

Magnetic pogo-pin contacts sit flush with the device's silicone or plastic exterior surface. Because there is no cavity, any surface moisture can be wiped dry with a simple towel in one second. Furthermore, BOHOL utilizes beryllium-copper internal springs encased in solid brass barrels plated with 0.5-micron pure gold. Gold is chemically noble and will not oxidize or corrode even when subjected to chlorine, sweat, or saline fluids.
