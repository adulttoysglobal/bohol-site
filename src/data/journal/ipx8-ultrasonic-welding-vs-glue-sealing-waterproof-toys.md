## 1. Why Bathwater Destroys Conventionally Glued Toys

Warm bathwater mixed with soaps and surfactants drastically lowers surface tension, allowing water molecules to penetrate hairline adhesive gaps around charging ports and control buttons. Once moisture reaches the lithium-polymer battery PCB, dendritic copper corrosion causes short circuits and thermal hazards.
