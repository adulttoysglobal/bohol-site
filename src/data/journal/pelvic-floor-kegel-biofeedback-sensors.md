## How Do Piezoresistive Force Sensors Measure Pelvic Contraction Vectors?

Unlike passive weighted Kegel balls that provide zero feedback, BOHOL's intelligent trainer embeds four micro-thin piezoresistive sensor arrays arranged in a 360-degree radial pattern along the anatomical probe shaft. When the pubococcygeus (PC) muscle contracts, mechanical pressure alters the electrical resistance of the conductive polymer matrix. An integrated 24-bit analog-to-digital converter (ADC) samples pressure changes at 100 Hz, mapping the exact force vector and duration of the pelvic contraction.

## How Does Gamified Biofeedback Transform Long-Term Exercise Compliance?

### How soon after childbirth can a woman begin using a smart Kegel trainer?
Women should always consult their OB-GYN or pelvic health physical therapist at their standard 6-week postpartum checkup before introducing any internal biofeedback device.

### Is the device suitable for women experiencing stress urinary incontinence?
Yes. Strengthening the levator ani and pubococcygeus muscles via guided biofeedback is the primary clinical first-line therapy for mild-to-moderate stress urinary incontinence (SUI).
