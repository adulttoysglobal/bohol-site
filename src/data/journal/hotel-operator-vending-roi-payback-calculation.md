## 1. Unit Economics: Breakdown of a 100-Room Hotel

Consider a 100-room hotel running at 72% average annual occupancy (72 occupied rooms per night). With conservative telemetry indicating an 18% purchasing rate (roughly 13 room purchases per night) and an average guest cart value of $22, the property generates $286 in daily gross sales, or $8,580 monthly. At an average product margin of 72%, the monthly net gross profit to the hotel operator is **$6,177**.

## 2. Payback Period Modeling Under 90 Days

Deploying 100 smart wall-mount or nightstand units represents an initial investment of approximately $18,000 for hardware and initial inventory. With net monthly operating profits of $6,177, the entire capital outlay is fully amortized in **2.9 months**. From month 4 onwards, the kiosk fleet generates pure passive profit.
