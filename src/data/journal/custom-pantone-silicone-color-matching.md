## Why Does Silicone Change Color During the Vulcanization Process?

Liquid silicone rubber (LSR) is supplied as a two-part liquid (Part A base silicone and Part B cross-linking platinum catalyst). Liquid color pigments are added during dynamic static mixing. However, when the silicone enters the 165°C heated mold, the platinum cross-linking reaction alters the optical refractive index of the polymer. A pigment formulation that looks perfect in liquid form will shift darker or more yellow after baking. BOHOL's formulation lab pre-calculates this thermal color shift, adjusting pigment ratios so the final cured product matches the target Pantone swatch precisely.

## What Is Delta-E (ΔE) Metrology and How Does It Guarantee Color Consistency?

In industrial colorimetry, Delta-E (ΔE) measures the mathematical distance between two colors in the CIE L*a*b* color space. A ΔE value greater than 2.0 is readily noticeable to the untrained human eye as a mismatched color. BOHOL enforces a strict tolerance threshold of ΔE < 0.8 across all production batches, ensuring that a device manufactured in January matches an accessory dock manufactured in October with absolute optical uniformity.
