## 1. When Private Label Beats Full OEM

Over 80% of successful sexual wellness startups generate their first $500,000 in revenue using Private Label or light ODM models. Because customer acquisition (content, influencers, retail placement) requires heavy cash flow, tying up $20,000 in unproven steel molds before validating your audience is the #1 mistake new founders make.

| Comparison Dimension | Private Label (White Label) | ODM Customization | Full Custom OEM |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tooling / Mold Cost** | $0 | $1,500 – $4,000 (Minor inserts) | $6,000 – $18,000+ (Full CNC mold) |
| **Minimum Order (MOQ)** | 100 – 300 units | 500 units | 1,000+ units |
| **Time to First Shipment** | 12 – 18 days | 25 – 35 days | 45 – 65 days |
| **Design Exclusivity / IP** | Shared hardware, unique brand | Semi-exclusive features/app | 100% exclusive mold & patent IP |

## 2. When to Graduate to Full Custom OEM Tooling

Once a category hero SKU consistently sells 500+ units per month or you are pitching national pharmacy chains and major European distributors who demand exclusive industrial design, transitioning to full OEM tooling locks competitors out of Amazon Buy Boxes and protects 75%+ gross margins long-term.
