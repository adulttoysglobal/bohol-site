## How Does Aesthetic Packaging Design Influence Guest Purchasing Behavior?

In high-tier hospitality, product presentation dictates perceived value. When intimate wellness kits mimic luxury apothecary cosmetics (like Aesop or Le Labo) with unbranded monochromatic sleeves, tactile textured papers, and discreet French-crease seals, purchase hesitation evaporates. Guests feel pampered rather than embarrassed, allowing operators to price 3-piece curated sets between $35 and $65 with zero price resistance.
