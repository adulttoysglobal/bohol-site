## 1. Why Standard Retail Packaging Fails in Vending

Retail packages designed for store pegboards often feature hanging tab holes, flimsy 250 GSM paperboard, or protruding plastic blister bubbles. Inside an automatic dispensing spiral, hanging tabs catch on the metal wire coil, causing the package to rotate upside down and jam. BOHOL manufactures all hotel vending SKUs in clean, rigid rectangular cartons with flush corners and zero protruding tabs.

## 2. Friction Coefficients: Gloss vs. Matte Coatings

When two glossy UV-coated boxes sit stacked against each other under summer humidity, high surface energy causes them to adhere ('blocking'). When the motor turns, the coil pushes both boxes at once, leading to a double-vend or a severe mechanical jam. BOHOL uses a specialized water-based anti-static matte lacquer that maintains a steady kinetic friction coefficient (µ = 0.22), ensuring smooth, predictable single-unit drops.
