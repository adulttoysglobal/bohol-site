## What Mechanical Factors Generate Unwanted Noise in Intimate Devices?

Most people assume a noisy vibrator simply has a cheap motor. In reality, the primary culprit is structural acoustic resonance. When a high-speed micro-motor (spinning at 8,000 to 12,000 RPM) is screwed directly into a hard ABS plastic shell, the shell acts as an acoustic soundboard—amplifying the motor's micro-vibrations just like a violin's hollow wooden body amplifies strings. Secondary noise stems from loose tolerances between eccentric brass weights and drive shafts.

## How Does Floating Silicone Suspension Isolate Motor Resonance?

To decouple mechanical energy, BOHOL engineers house motors inside a proprietary Shore 40A liquid silicone damping boot before inserting them into structural chassis bulkheads. This elastomeric isolator absorbs high-frequency acoustic harmonics, preventing mechanical vibration from transmitting into the exterior housing. Only the low-frequency tactile energy travels through the thick outer silicone skin directly to the user's body.
