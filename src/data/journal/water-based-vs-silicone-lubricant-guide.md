## 1. Why Silicone Lube Destroys Silicone Toys

In basic chemistry, 'like dissolves like.' Liquid silicone lubricant consists of linear dimethicone siloxane molecules. When applied to cured silicone elastomeric toys (like [Harmony Rabbit](/products/harmony-rabbit/) or [Bobi III](/products/bobi-iii/)), the liquid siloxane migrates into the polymer matrix, breaking down the cross-linked bonds. The surface of the toy becomes sticky, gummy, and permanently pitted. For any silicone toy, stick strictly to high-viscosity water-based gels like [NR-LUBR-1](/products/nr-lubr-1/).

## 2. When to Choose Silicone or Hybrid Formulas

Silicone lubricants are hydrophobic—they do not absorb into skin or wash away in water. This makes silicone lube the ultimate choice for shower sex, hot tub play, or extended anal intercourse where natural moisture is absent. It stays slippery for hours without drying out, though it requires warm soap and water to wash off.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use saliva as a substitute for lubricant?
Saliva contains digestive enzymes (amylase) and oral bacteria that can disrupt vaginal flora, triggering yeast infections or bacterial vaginosis. Furthermore, saliva dries out within seconds, causing increased friction.

### Why do some water-based lubes get sticky after 5 minutes?
Cheap formulas use glycerin or propylene glycol, which dry into a tacky, sugary residue as water evaporates. Premium formulas use sodium hyaluronate or cellulose, which remain slippery or reactivate instantly with a drop of water.
