Conversely, if molded from high-durometer firm silicone, it feels hard, artificial, and uncomfortable against human tissue.

## How Does Multi-Shot LSR Tooling Chemically Fuse Two Different Silicone Hardnesses?

Unlike gluing two pieces of rubber together (which always fails and delaminates over time), multi-shot dual-density injection relies on chemical covalent bonding during sequential vulcanization. The structural core is injected first into an S136 stainless steel mold at 160°C and allowed to partially cure to a 90% cross-linked state. A secondary automated rotary tool table shifts the core into a larger mold cavity, where the ultra-soft outer silicone formulation is injected. The polymers cross-link across the interface, creating an unbreakable, monolithic bond.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can the soft outer skin peel away from the firmer inner core over time?
No. Because both layers are platinum-cured liquid silicone rubber bonded during high-heat vulcanization, the interface is fused at the molecular siloxane level; the material will tear before it delaminates along the seam.

### Are dual-density silicone toys compatible with boiling water sterilization?
Yes. Both silicone formulations are thermoset elastomers stable up to 200°C and can be safely sanitized in boiling water without separation or hardening.
