## 1. Molecular Structure & Chemical Permeability

One of the most frequently asked questions in intimate hygiene forums is: *'Can I sanitize my device with rubbing alcohol?'* The answer depends fundamentally on the polymer chemistry of the device.

Thermoplastic Elastomer (TPE) is a physical blend of polystyrene hard segments and polybutadiene soft rubber plasticized with up to 70% mineral oil. When alcohol contacts TPE, it acts as a solvent, stripping away the oil plasticizers. Within minutes, the material becomes sticky, brittle, and emits strong chemical odors.

In sharp contrast, Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR) undergoes platinum-catalyzed hydrosilylation, creating an unbreakable covalent silicon-oxygen (Si-O-Si) polymer backbone. This cross-linked molecular network resists alcohols, mild acids, and temperatures ranging from -40°C to +200°C.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the recommended cleaning protocol for BOHOL liquid silicone products?

Wash the IPX7/IPX8 waterproof device with warm water and antibacterial foaming soap for 30 seconds, then pat dry with a clean microfiber cloth. For clinical sterilization, silicone portions can be dipped in boiling water for 60 seconds without risk of warping.

### Are BOHOL silicone products compatible with silicone-based lubricants?

Like all 100% pure silicone items, prolonged direct contact with silicone-based oils can cause surface softening. We strictly recommend high-viscosity water-based lubricants, such as our pharmaceutical-grade H2O formulas.
