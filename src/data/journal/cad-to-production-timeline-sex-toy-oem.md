## What Critical Adjustments Occur During the Design for Manufacturability (DFM) Phase?

Industrial designers frequently create beautiful 3D renderings that are impossible to mold without parting line defects or silicone flash. During BOHOL's initial 5-day DFM phase, our tooling engineers analyze draft angles (minimum 1.5° to 2° for clean de-molding), wall thickness uniformity (preventing sink marks), and optimal gate placement. Concurrently, our electrical engineering team layouts the internal PCB schematic, optimizing component placement to maximize battery volume inside the ergonomic shell.

## How Does Precision 5-Axis CNC Milling Ensure Seamless Parting Lines in Steel Molds?

The tactile feel of a high-end silicone product depends on the precision of the mold parting line. If mold halves mismatch by even 0.03mm, sharp silicone flash is created that scratches human tissue. BOHOL cuts hardened S136 stainless steel tooling blocks using German DMG MORI 5-axis CNC machining centers and Sodick mirror-spark EDM, holding tooling tolerances within ±0.008mm. The mold cavities are hand-polished to an optical SPI A-1 diamond finish, resulting in an ultra-silky, flash-free silicone exterior.
