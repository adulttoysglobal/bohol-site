## 1. The 'Wallet Hazard': Why It Destroys Condoms

Carrying a condom in a leather wallet tucked into your back pocket subjects the foil pouch to continuous mechanical flexing, friction from walking, and radiant body heat (37°C). Within a few weeks, the airtight seal degrades or pinholes develop in the aluminum laminate foil. The internal lubricant dries out, and atmospheric oxygen oxidizes the latex, turning it brittle and prone to tearing upon the first thrust.

## 2. Ideal Storage Environments: The 25°C Rule

Store condoms in a cool, dark drawer, nightstand cabinet, or dedicated travel tin maintained between 15°C and 25°C (59°F–77°F). Keep them away from direct sunlight, radiators, bathroom humidity steam, and sharp items like keys or nail clippers in your bag.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I tell if a condom is still good to use in the dark?
Before tearing the wrapper, gently pinch the foil between your thumb and index finger. You should feel a distinct air cushion and a slippery pocket of lubricant. If the foil feels completely flat, air has escaped and the condom should be discarded.

### What happens if you use an expired condom?
Expired condoms have dried lubricant and degraded elastomeric chains, meaning their likelihood of snapping or tearing during intercourse increases by over 400%.
