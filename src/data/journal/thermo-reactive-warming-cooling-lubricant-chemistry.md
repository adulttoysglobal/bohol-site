## How Does Vanillyl Butyl Ether (VBE) Stimulate TRPV1 Thermoreceptors Without Tissue Damage?

Human skin senses warmth through Transient Receptor Potential Vanilloid 1 (TRPV1) ion channels, typically activated by temperatures above 43°C. Unlike capsicum, which binds irreversibly and causes severe tissue inflammation and erythema, Vanillyl Butyl Ether (VBE) is an engineered aroma ether that gently activates TRPV1 channels at ambient temperature. It produces an authentic sensation of radiating warmth (approx. 40°C sensation) that develops over 60 seconds and lasts 20 minutes with zero thermal tissue burning.
