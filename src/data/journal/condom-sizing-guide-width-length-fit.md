Finding your true condom size is not about ego—it is about matching nominal width to your penis circumference for effortless comfort.

## 1. How to Measure Circumference Accurately

Length is rarely the cause of condom failure, as almost all commercial condoms unroll to 180mm–200mm (7.5 to 8 inches), which accommodates 98% of men. The crucial measurement is girth (circumference). Wrap a flexible measuring tape or a strip of paper around the thickest part of your erect shaft. Mark the overlap point, measure the distance in millimeters, and divide by 2 to determine your ideal flat nominal width.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I do if a condom slips off inside my partner?
Gently retrieve the condom using clean fingers. If there is any chance semen escaped, seek emergency contraception (Plan B) within 72 hours and consider STI screening.

### Why does the base ring feel so tight even when the tip fits fine?
Some condoms feature aggressively tight base bands to prevent roll-off. Switching to a flared shape with an expanded base ring relieves restrictive constriction around the pubic bone.
