## 1. Thermal Conductivity: The Warmth of True Skin-on-Skin

The primary complaint about standard latex condoms is not just thickness; it is thermal insulation. Latex is an insulator that blocks body heat, making penetration feel cold and synthetic. Polyurethane has an exceptionally high thermal conductivity coefficient. When using [NR-COND-1](/products/nr-cond-1/), the warmth of internal mucosal tissue transfers through the polymer membrane within two seconds, creating a warm, organic sensation that feels virtually identical to unprotected intimacy.

## 2. Tensile Strength and Latex Allergy Elimination

Natural rubber contains antigenic proteins that cause burning, itching, and rashes in up to 4.3% of the population. Polyurethane is an inert medical-grade synthetic polymer completely devoid of organic plant proteins. Under laboratory burst-pressure tests, 0.01mm polyurethane withstands higher puncture forces from fingernails than standard latex, ensuring robust safety despite its gossamer thinness.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are 0.01mm condoms more prone to breaking than thicker condoms?
No. Polyurethane has a much higher molecular tensile strength than latex, passing ISO 23409 burst volume and pressure tests with exceptional margins.

### Do polyurethane condoms require special sizing?
Because polyurethane stretches less elastically than latex, choosing the correct nominal width (52mm vs 54mm) is important to ensure a snug, comfortable fit without constriction.
