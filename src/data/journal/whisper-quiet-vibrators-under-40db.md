## 1. Why Cheap Toys Buzz While Premium Toys Hum

The loud rattling noise of cheap vibrators comes from low-cost iron-core motors clamped rigidly against hollow plastic bodies. As the eccentric counterweight spins, mechanical harmonics cause the entire shell to vibrate like an acoustic speaker cone. In BOHOL's [Bite](/products/bite/) and [Xiaoqiao](/products/xiaoqiao/), the motor is cradled inside a 20A durometer silicone isolation damper. Furthermore, the external casing is molded with thick-walled ABS bonded to liquid silicone, dampening resonance by over 65%.
