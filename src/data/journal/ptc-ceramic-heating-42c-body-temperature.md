## What Is the Self-Regulating Physics of Positive Temperature Coefficient (PTC) Ceramics?

Unlike standard nichrome heating wires whose resistance remains constant regardless of temperature (meaning they will continue heating until they melt or catch fire if a controller fails), PTC ceramic stones exhibit a radical resistance jump at their Curie point (typically 42°C to 44°C). As the ceramic warms up to 42°C, its electrical resistance surges exponentially by thousands of ohms, automatically choking off electric current. It physically cannot overheat beyond its Curie temperature, providing fail-safe thermal safety.

## How Does Uniform Silicone Thermal Distribution Prevent Hot Spots?
