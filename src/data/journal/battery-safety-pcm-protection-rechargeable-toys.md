## 2. Preventing Deep Discharge Dormancy

Consumers often leave adult toys sitting in nightstand drawers for 6 to 9 months without recharging. If battery voltage drops below 2.5V, copper shunts can form, ruining the battery. BOHOL’s ultra-low quiescent current circuitry (< 5 µA) allows toys to remain in storage for over 18 months without entering destructive deep-discharge states.
