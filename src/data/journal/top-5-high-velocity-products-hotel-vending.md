## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should we include novelty gag gifts in the vending matrix?
No. Data shows novelty candy or cheap plastic gags have high return rates and damage a hotel’s upscale reputation. Stick to premium, practical personal wellness essentials.

### What packaging format works best inside spiral dispensers?
Rigid paperboard boxes with standardized rectangular dimensions (e.g., 70 × 110 × 25 mm) prevent drops from snagging or jamming in spiral coils.
