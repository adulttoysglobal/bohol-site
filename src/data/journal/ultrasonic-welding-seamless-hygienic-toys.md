## How Does Ultrasonic Acoustic Energy Fuse Plastic at the Molecular Level?

An ultrasonic welding press converts 50/60 Hz electrical current into high-frequency 20 kHz mechanical vibrations via a piezoelectric transducer. This vibration is focused through a custom-machined titanium sonotrode horn directly onto an engineered triangular 'energy director' ridge molded along the plastic mating seam. The friction generates localized frictional heat (over 220°C in 0.3 seconds), melting the energy director and fusing the two polycarbonate halves into a single monolithic component.

## Why Are Screw Holes and Chemical Adhesives Unacceptable in Medical-Grade Novelties?

Chemical glues break down over time when exposed to silicone lubricants, body oils, and hot bathwater, eventually leaking water into internal motor electronics. Furthermore, screw holes require soft rubber plug covers that can pop out, swallow bacteria, and present choking or pinching hazards. Ultrasonic welding creates a flush, completely seamless, hermetically sealed perimeter that can be scrubbed under running water with zero risk of bacterial harbor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can ultrasonic welding be used on all types of plastics?
Ultrasonic welding is ideal for amorphous engineering thermoplastics like Polycarbonate (PC), ABS, and PC/ABS blends. Semi-crystalline plastics require specialized ultrasonic frequency tuning which our tooling engineers calibrate precisely.

### How is weld integrity verified on the production line?
Immediately following the ultrasonic weld cycle, our automated line subjects 100% of housings to a differential pressure vacuum decay test, verifying airtight hermetic integrity before internal electronics are powered.
