Compact products can take very different forms. A small overall size does not mean two devices share the same controls, construction or care requirements.

## The body and its stand

[Mengqu](/products/mengqu/) uses a cat-inspired silhouette with a matching stand. Its specification lists dimensions of 96 × 69 × 54 mm and a weight of 105.2 g. White and pink variants are listed under different model numbers.

The stand is part of the visible product arrangement. Do not assume it is a charging dock unless the instructions for your exact model describe it that way.

## A protective cover

[Bite](/products/bite/) has a rounded body and a separate protective cover. The supplied photographs show the product with the cover both on and off, helping you understand the complete exterior.

[Summer Rabbit](/products/summer-rabbit/) pairs its curved body with a case. Its pink and lilac versions share a recognizable design language.

## Color and construction are separate questions

When choosing a variant, confirm both the color and the model information. A color option does not automatically establish a difference—or equivalence—in technical specifications.
