## 1. Hermetic Sealing Technologies: Ultrasonic Welding vs Liquid Silicone Overmolding

Achieving true hermetic isolation between high-power lithium batteries, vibration motors, and the external aquatic environment requires advanced industrial joining methods:

- **20 kHz Ultrasonic Plastic Welding:** For internal ABS/PC chassis halves, BOHOL uses Branson ultrasonic welding generators. Mechanical vibrations at 20,000 Hz melt energy directors within 0.35 seconds, creating molecular fusion across the entire joint seam without messy, volatile chemical glues.
- **Multi-Shot Liquid Silicone Overmolding (Co-Molding):** The welded internal core is transferred into a secondary injection mold where platinum-cured liquid silicone rubber is injected directly over the rigid sub-chassis. The silicone forms a continuous, seamless skin with zero mechanical parting lines.
- **Magnetic Hermetic Pogo-Pin Charging:** Traditional barrel jacks require physical rubber plugs that users inevitably lose. BOHOL integrates 24K gold-plated magnetic pogo-pin terminals sealed with internal O-rings, preserving waterproof integrity for the life of the product.

## 2. 100% Production-Line Air Decay Pressure Leak Testing

Destructive water immersion testing cannot be performed on 100% of finished sellable goods. Instead, BOHOL utilizes automated dry air decay leak detectors:

1. **Chamber Pressurization:** Finished devices are placed inside custom contoured chambers pressurized to 30 kPa.
2. **Differential Pressure Sensing:** Sensitive pressure transducers measure differential pressure variance over a 15-second dwell time. Any pressure drop exceeding 0.05 kPa indicates microscopic air escape through seals, triggering instant robotic rejection.
3. **Hot Water Expansion Stress Test:** Random batch audits submerge devices in 45°C water while internal motors cycle at maximum frequency to verify that internal air thermal expansion does not compromise perimeter gaskets.
