## 1. Why Specifying 'Soft Silicone' Causes Batch Disasters

Words like 'soft' or 'firm' mean completely different things to different factory technicians. Without specifying an exact ASTM D2240 Shore durometer target on your technical pack, one batch of vibrators can arrive rock-hard while the next feels flimsy and tears around the power button.
