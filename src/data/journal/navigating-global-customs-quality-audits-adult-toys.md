## 1. US FDA Novelty Classification & Customs Declarations

Unlike prescription medical devices, commercial intimate massagers and strokers imported into the United States are declared under Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) Code **8509.80.5095** (Electromechanical domestic appliances). To avoid regulatory delays, packaging and documentation must state clearly: 'Sold as an adult novelty for sensual massage; not intended for medical, therapeutic, or prosthetic use.'

## 2. European RoHS & REACH Chemical Audits

European customs authorities actively test adult products for banned plasticizers and heavy metals (lead, cadmium, mercury, hexavalent chromium). Every component used in BOHOL’s manufacturing campus—from internal solder wires to outer silicone skins—is tested by SGS and certified 100% compliant with **EU Directive 2011/65/EU (RoHS 2.0)** and **REACH Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006**.
