## What Tactile and Sensory Elements Define a Luxury Unboxing Experience?

Luxury packaging engages multiple sensory modalities. Visually, BOHOL incorporates understated, architectural graphic design, gold or rose-gold hot foil stamping, and spot UV varnishes. Tactilely, outer boxes are coated with imported soft-touch matte film that feels smooth and velvety, mirroring the medical liquid silicone inside. Structurally, drawer-style boxes feature custom-calibrated atmospheric air resistance: the inner tray glides open with a smooth, 2-second pneumatic drop.

## How Does Precision EVA Foam Interior Tooling Protect Fragile Electronics?

During international air and ground parcel delivery, packages endure severe drops and vibration. Flimsy thermoformed PVC plastic blister trays crack easily and allow hardware to rattle loose. BOHOL cuts high-density (38-degree) closed-cell EVA foam on precision waterjet and CNC routing tables. The foam cavity is laser-contoured to the exact CAD millimeter silhouette of the product, protecting delicate motors, LCD screens, and optical sensors against 1.5-meter drops.
