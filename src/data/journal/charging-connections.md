Charging information is easy to overlook while comparing product photographs. It is also one of the details that can vary between similar-looking devices.

## Start with the product name

The current product sheets list USB-C charging for [Twist & Fun](/products/twist-fun/), [CJ7](/products/cj7/) and [Space](/products/space/). [Starship](/products/starship/) lists a DC socket instead. Keep the exact model name with your charging accessories so they are not confused with those for another device.

## The connector is only part of the specification

A matching plug does not, by itself, confirm the power requirements. Check the product manual for the required charging supply and use the accessories intended for that model. Do not use a different charger simply because its connector appears to fit.

## Read time estimates in context

Twist & Fun lists three hours for charging. CJ7 lists four hours. Starship lists 75 minutes. These values come from separate product sheets and are not a controlled comparison of charging performance.

Listed runtime can also depend on the selected modes and operating conditions. Treat it as product information, rather than a guarantee for every setting and every use.

## Keep the instructions accessible

Retain the manual and packaging information that identify your model. If an accessory is missing or needs replacement, prepare the model name and order details before contacting your seller.
