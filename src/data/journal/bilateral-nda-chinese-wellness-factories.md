## What Is the Critical Difference Between a Western NDA and a Chinese NNN Agreement?

A standard American NDA prevents a factory from telling others about your design, but it does not explicitly prevent the factory from *using* the design to manufacture and sell the product directly under another shell brand. An NNN agreement covers three separate covenants: 1. Non-Disclosure (keeps information confidential); 2. Non-Use (strictly prohibits the factory from using your 3D CAD files or tooling for any production outside your purchase orders); 3. Non-Circumvention (prohibits the factory from selling directly to your clients or distributors).

## Why Are the Corporate Red Seal (Chop) and Local Arbitration Clauses Mandatory?

Under Chinese Civil Code Contract Law, an agreement signed only with an individual manager's English signature has dubious legal standing. To be legally binding, the agreement must be stamped with the company's official registered corporate red seal (Gongzhang / 公章), which is registered with the State Administration for Market Regulation. Furthermore, designating CIETAC (China International Economic and Trade Arbitration Commission) in Shenzhen or Beijing ensures that arbitral awards can be enforced immediately against factory domestic bank assets.
