Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR) is universally recognized as the gold standard material for premium intimate wellness devices due to its complete chemical inertness, velvet tactile finish, and human biocompatibility. However, silicone’s electrostatically charged surface attracts airborne particulates with extreme velocity during uncured liquid states. Maintaining pristine optical clarity and zero particulate contamination requires Class 100,000 (ISO 14644-1 Class 8) dust-free cleanroom environments.

## 1. Cleanroom Facility Architecture & Environmental Controls

At BOHOL’s 35,000 m² manufacturing campus, our silicone injection workshops operate under continuous positive pressure micro-climate controls:

- **Triple-Stage HEPA Air Filtration:** Primary pre-filters, secondary medium-efficiency bags, and terminal H14 HEPA filters capture 99.995% of airborne particles down to 0.3 micrometers ($\mu	ext{m}$).
- **Positive Differential Air Pressure:** Rooms maintain a minimum positive differential pressure of +15 Pascals relative to outside corridors, ensuring that air always rushes outward whenever interlocked airlock doors cycle.
- **Air Exchange Rates:** Cleanrooms cycle over 25 complete volumetric air changes per hour, preventing volatile organic chemical accumulation.

| **Temperature Regulation** | Ambient (15°C – 35°C) | **Stabilized at 22°C $\pm 1°C$** |

## 2. Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR) Dosing & Tooling Precision

Unlike standard compression molding using solid silicone gum cakes, BOHOL utilizes automated two-component LSR injection systems (Component A base and Component B platinum catalyst):

1. **Closed-Loop Gravimetric Dosing:** Pneumatically controlled metering pumps deliver exact 1:1 volumetric ratios with dynamic pigment injection directly into high-pressure water-cooled static mixers.
2. **Cold-Runner Mold Tooling:** Heated mold cavities (170°C – 195°C) cross-link the silicone in under 25 seconds, while chilled cold-runner nozzles keep raw liquid silicone at 20°C, eliminating runner waste.
3. **Automated Vacuum De-Gassing:** Prior to shot injection, mold cavities are evacuated to -98 kPa within 0.8 seconds to eliminate micro-bubbles and surface pitting.
