## 1. The 3-Step Cleaning Protocol

1. **Rinse:** If your device is rated IPX7 waterproof (like [Summer Rabbit](/products/summer-rabbit/)), rinse the entire body under lukewarm running water.<br>2. **Sanitize:** Apply an alcohol-free, antibacterial intimate toy cleanser or a gentle, fragrance-free foaming soap. Gently lather the silicone surface with your fingertips, paying special attention to seams, buttons, and suction nozzles.<br>3. **Rinse & Dry:** Rinse off all lather thoroughly. Pat dry with a fresh microfiber towel and allow to air-dry in a well-ventilated space.

## 2. Storage Rules: Protecting the Velvet Silicone Finish

Silicone possesses an electro-static property that attracts airborne dust, pet hair, and lint. Storing your device loose in a drawer is a fast way to soil the material. Furthermore, if two silicone toys touch each other in storage, plasticizers and siloxanes can migrate between them, causing the contact points to melt and fuse together. Always store each device in its own breathable satin or cotton drawstring pouch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use baby wipes to clean my vibrator in bed?
Unscented, water-based baby wipes are acceptable for a quick wipe down immediately after sex, but you should still give the device a proper wash with warm water and toy cleaner before long-term storage.

### What happens if a toy is left uncharged for a year?
Lithium-ion cells naturally self-discharge. If left empty for months, the cell enters 'deep discharge' mode, causing the circuit board to lock out and permanently rendering the toy unable to charge.
