## 1. Can You Clean an Air-Pulse or Silicone Device with Rubbing Alcohol?

While solid, non-electric 100% platinum-cured silicone resists brief surface contact with 70% isopropyl alcohol, **rechargeable motorized devices and air-pulse suction nozzles are built differently**. They combine thin molded silicone nozzles, velvet matte tactile surface treatments, internal acoustic membranes, and magnetic charging seals. Repeated rubbing alcohol soaking strips the silky matte finish, leaves sticky white residue, and can gradually dry out waterproof O-rings.

For reliable 99.9% surface sanitization without material wear, always use warm water paired with an **alcohol-free, pH-balanced foaming cleanser**.

## 2. The 3-Step Daily Cleaning & Nozzle Care Protocol

1. **Pre-Rinse:** If your device carries an IPX7 or IPX8 waterproof rating (like [Summer Rabbit](/products/summer-rabbit/) or [Bobi III](/products/bobi-iii/)), rinse the exterior and nozzle rim under lukewarm running water before residue dries.
2. **Gentle Lather:** Apply 1–2 pumps of alcohol-free antibacterial foaming cleanser or fragrance-free mild soap. Work gently around control buttons, silicone seams, and suction nozzle openings with clean fingertips for 20–30 seconds.
3. **Thorough Rinse & Air-Dry:** Rinse away all soap completely, pat dry using a lint-free microfiber cloth, and allow the device to air-dry fully in a ventilated spot before placing it into storage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I clean my air-pulse suction device with 70% rubbing alcohol?

Routine alcohol cleaning is not recommended for motorized or air-pulse devices because alcohol dries out matte UV silicone coatings and accelerates wear on thin nozzle seals. Use warm running water and an alcohol-free foaming cleanser instead.

### Is it safe to boil a waterproof silicone wellness device to sanitize it?

Only solid, non-electric 100% platinum-cured silicone items can be boiled for 3–5 minutes. Never boil rechargeable or battery-powered devices, as boiling heat permanently damages lithium cells and circuit boards.

### Can I use baby wipes or hand soap for a quick clean?

Unscented, alcohol-free water-based wipes work for a quick surface wipe immediately after use, followed by a proper lukewarm water and mild cleanser rinse before storage. Avoid scented hand soaps containing heavy oils or moisturizers.

### What happens if a rechargeable device is left uncharged for a year?

Lithium-ion batteries naturally self-discharge slowly over time. Recharging stored devices every 60 to 90 days prevents deep-discharge protection lockout and extends battery health past 500 cycles.
