## Why Do Pad Printing Inks Inevitably Peel Off Silicone Devices?

Liquid silicone rubber has extremely low surface energy and natural non-stick properties. For ink to adhere, the silicone must be subjected to open-flame corona treatment, primed with toxic silane coupling agents, and baked in high-heat ovens. Even with these harsh chemicals, repeated exposure to warm soapy water, skin friction, and personal lubricants dissolves the ink matrix. The printed logo rubs off like cheap paint, leaving an ugly scarred surface and exposing users to flaking pigment flakes.

## How Does Cold UV Fiber Laser Etching Create Crisp, Permanent Typography?

BOHOL utilizes advanced 355nm ultraviolet (UV) 'cold' lasers. Because UV photons have high energy, they break chemical molecular bonds directly through photo-chemical ablation rather than burning the material with thermal heat. This creates an ultra-crisp, micro-textured, light-scattering matte finish on the silicone with zero burning, zero charred edges, and zero foreign chemicals. The logo is physically part of the silicone itself and can never peel, fade, or rub off.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can laser engraving achieve multi-color logos on silicone?
Laser engraving creates a subtle, elegant tonal contrast (frosting the silicone surface to a clean matte white or light grey shade). For brands desiring multi-color logos, we recommend embedding contrasting molded silicone inserts or applying laser marking to the polished metallic electroplated charging base.

### Does laser etching create rough, abrasive edges that could irritate skin?
No. Our 355nm cold UV lasers produce microscopic etching depths under 40 microns, resulting in a velvety soft-touch texture that is completely smooth and non-irritating to touch.
