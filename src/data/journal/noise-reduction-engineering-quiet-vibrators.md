## 1. The 3 Acoustic Transmission Pathways

Unwanted noise in personal devices travels through three pathways: 1. **Airborne Sound:** Emitted directly from spinning motor coils; 2. **Structure-Borne Vibration:** The vibrating motor transmitting kinetic shock into the rigid plastic shell; 3. **Surface Resonance:** The plastic casing acting like a guitar body, amplifying internal sound outward. Addressing all three pathways is the only way to achieve true whisper-quiet performance.

## 2. Silicone Isolation Cradles: Decoupling the Drive Train

In products like [Bite](/products/bite/) and [Xiaoqiao](/products/xiaoqiao/), the motor does not contact the outer casing directly. Instead, it is suspended inside a bespoke 20A durometer silicone cradle. This elastomeric isolator absorbs high-frequency vibrations (above 150 Hz) that cause buzzing, while permitting deep, low-frequency waves (50–90 Hz) to pass through to the erogenous zones.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How are decibel levels verified during production?
Every new production lot is tested inside an ISO 3744 certified anechoic chamber using calibrated Brüel & Kjær sound level meters positioned exactly 1 meter from the device.

### Does motor dampening reduce vibration intensity felt by the user?
No. By dampening only high-frequency acoustic rattle, all mechanical energy is concentrated into deep, penetrating low-frequency tissue stimulation.
