## 1. Overcoming RF Signal Attenuation in Wearable Intimate Hardware

Human tissue is roughly 60% water, creating intense 2.4 GHz radio frequency attenuation. Cheap wearable devices using PCB trace antennas frequently disconnect the moment a user sits down or wears denim.

BOHOL isolates a dedicated ceramic chip antenna away from battery shielding and incorporates BLE 5.3 coded PHY modes to double effective link budget without draining lithium cells.
