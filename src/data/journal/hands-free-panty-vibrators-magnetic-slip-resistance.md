## How Do Neodymium Magnetic Flux Lines Secure Hardware Through Dense Underwear Fabrics?

Standard magnets lack the magnetic flux density required to clamp securely through thick multi-ply fabrics like denim or double-lined cotton underwear. BOHOL pairs sintered Neodymium-Iron-Boron (NdFeB N52 grade) magnetic cores with custom-shaped flux concentrator steel plates. This focuses magnetic field lines directly through underwear fibers without leakage, achieving over 20 Newtons of lateral shear resistance while remaining completely gentle on fabric weaves.

## Why Is Full Silicone Hermetic Encapsulation Essential for Wearable Magnets?
