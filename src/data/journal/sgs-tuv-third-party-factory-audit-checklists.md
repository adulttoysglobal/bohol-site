## What Are the 5 Critical Inspection Sections in an SGS Quality Audit?

1. Legal Entity & Land Title: Checking business licenses, tax records, and real estate ownership to verify the company legally operates the 35,000 m² campus.<br/>2. Machinery & Tooling Registry: Inspecting serial numbers, maintenance logs, and calibration stickers on 5-axis DMG MORI CNC milling machines and cleanroom injection presses.<br/>3. Incoming Material Quality Control (IQC): Auditing raw material quarantine bays, silicone supplier Certificates of Analysis (COA), and battery batch records.<br/>4. In-Process Quality Control (IPQC): Observing cleanroom gowning air showers, robotic automated assembly jigs, and 100% vacuum testing tanks.<br/>5. Final Metrology & Traceability: Checking barcode serialization, retention samples, and quarantine cages for non-conforming items.

## Why Is Social Compliance (BSCI / SMETA) Mandatory for Major Western Retailers?

Major retail conglomerates (Boots, Target, Lovehoney, Spencer Gifts) refuse to stock products from factories that fail ethical labor audits. Social audits evaluate working hours, living wages, child labor prohibition, fire escape routes, dormitory sanitation, and personal protective equipment (PPE).
