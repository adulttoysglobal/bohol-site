## How Does Cube Utilization Differ Between Fragmented LCL and Full 40HQ Consolidation?

In fragmented LCL shipping, freight forwarders charge whichever is greater: cubic meters (CBM) or volumetric weight (1 CBM = 1,000 kg for ocean). Because lingerie and boxed items consume high cubic space without weight, while motorized strokers are dense and heavy, shipping them separately results in severe freight penalties. By engineering a 3D palletization matrix, BOHOL places dense silicone items on the floor tier and lightweight boxed consumables on top, achieving 94% volumetric fill rate inside a 40HQ container.

## What Customs Hazards Are Eliminated by Turnkey DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) Shipping?

Adult novelty products contain Class 9 dangerous goods (rechargeable lithium-ion batteries) and face rigorous customs inspection under local obscenity regulations. Under our DDP terms, BOHOL's licensed international logistics partners act as the Importer of Record (IOR), submitting compliant Harmonized System (HS 8509.80) codes, paying all local VAT/tariffs, and delivering directly to your domestic distribution center.
