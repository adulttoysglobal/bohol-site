## What Is the Hard Financial Math: Air Courier vs. Consolidated 20GP Ocean Container?

Consider a typical mid-sized order of 2,500 units of mixed adult hardware (gross weight approx. 1,800 kg; volume 14 CBM). Shipping this consignment via DHL/FedEx air express costs approximately $17,100 ($9.50/kg). The exact same consignment consolidated inside a 20GP ocean container via BOHOL turnkey DDP door-to-door ocean freight costs approximately $3,800 total ($2.11/kg). The distributor instantly pockets $13,300 in pure logistics savings on a single shipment.
