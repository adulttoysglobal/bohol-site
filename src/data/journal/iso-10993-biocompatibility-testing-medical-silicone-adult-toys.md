## 1. The Truth Behind 'Medical-Grade Silicone' Marketing Claims

Countless generic traders slap '100% Medical-Grade Silicone' onto product listings without a single laboratory dossier. Legitimate medical-grade status requires both certified platinum-cured raw polymers (such as Wacker or Dow biocompatible grades) AND controlled Class 100,000 cleanroom processing so airborne dust, mold-release sprays, or heavy-metal pigments never contaminate the finished surface.

| **ISO 10993-10 / 23** | Mucosal irritation & skin sensitization | Zero-rash proof for sensitive intimate contact |

## 2. How to Audit a Factory's Lab Reports in 60 Seconds

Always verify four elements on any supplier's compliance certificate: (1) accredited third-party lab header (SGS, TÜV, Intertek, or Bureau Veritas), (2) test sample photo matching the actual silicone compound, (3) valid recent issue date aligned with current REACH SVHC lists, and (4) legal manufacturer entity name matching the factory receiving your wire transfer.
