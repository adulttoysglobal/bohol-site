## What Neuro-Chemical Mechanism Enables Benzocaine to Prolong Male Stamina?

Premature ejaculation is frequently driven by hyper-sensitive dorsal penile nerve pathways. Benzocaine (ethyl 4-aminobenzoate) is a reversible local anesthetic that binds directly to voltage-gated sodium ion channels inside nerve axons. By blocking sodium ion influx, it inhibits the propagation of sensory action potentials to the central nervous system. At a precise 4% pharmaceutical concentration, it reduces hyper-acute peak friction sensations without numbing deep erectile pressure sensations.
