## 1. The 3 Hidden Factory Setup Costs Driving MOQ Tiers

Many new importers assume MOQs are arbitrary factory barriers. In reality, three physical production constraints dictate minimum batch sizes: (1) liquid silicone barrel purging requires flushing 3–5 kg of platinum elastomer every time a Pantone color changes, (2) SMT pick-and-place machines load resistors and Bluetooth chips on 1,000-unit reels, and (3) CMYK offset printing for foil-stamped magnetic gift boxes carries a fixed plate and die-cutting setup fee regardless of whether you print 200 or 2,000 boxes.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **3,000 – 10,000+ pcs** | Dedicated line + custom mold rebates | -30% to -38% | National wholesalers & enterprise OEM partners |

## 2. Smart Inventory Strategy: Mix Stock Colors with Custom Packaging

The smartest way to preserve cash flow while looking like a Tier-1 luxury brand is separating hardware MOQ from packaging MOQ. Order 1,000 custom-printed luxury boxes upfront (lowering box cost by 50%) and store excess flat-packed cartons free in BOHOL's warehouse while drawing down hardware in 200-unit batches.
