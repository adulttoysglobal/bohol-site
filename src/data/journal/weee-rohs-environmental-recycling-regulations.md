## What Toxic Chemicals Are Prohibited Under the EU RoHS 2.0 Directive?

Many budget electronics manufacturers still use cheap lead-based solders on PCB boards and PVC cables plasticized with banned phthalates. Under RoHS 2.0, lead (Pb), mercury (Hg), hexavalent chromium (Cr6+), polybrominated biphenyls (PBB), and four specific phthalates must be below 1,000 ppm (0.1%), with cadmium (Cd) restricted below 100 ppm (0.01%). BOHOL utilizes 100% lead-free SAC305 (Tin-Silver-Copper) surface-mount solder paste and non-phthalate silicone cables across all models.

## What Are Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) Registration Mandates in Germany and France?

Under German ElektroG and French AGEC laws, any brand selling consumer electronics directly to consumers (via Amazon.de, Shopify, or retail) must hold an official WEEE registration number from national registries (such as Stiftung EAR in Germany). Amazon and other e-commerce marketplaces automatically suspend seller listings that fail to upload a validated EPR registration number. BOHOL's regulatory division assists international distribution partners in completing local EPR filings smoothly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the crossed-out wheelie bin symbol required on the product itself or just the box?
Under European standards, the symbol must be visibly, legibly, and indelibly molded or laser-engraved directly on the physical hardware unless the device is too small (under 5cm), in which case it must be printed on the packaging and manual.

### How often does BOHOL renew laboratory RoHS test reports?
Our raw materials, silicone pigments, PCB boards, and solder supplies undergo comprehensive annual chemical re-testing through accredited international test laboratories (such as BACL and CCUT).
