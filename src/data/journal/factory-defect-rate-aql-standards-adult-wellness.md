## How Do AQL 0.65 and AQL 2.5 Sampling Plans Differ in Real Manufacturing Practice?

Under ISO 2859-1 (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4), the Acceptable Quality Limit defines the maximum percentage of non-conforming items considered satisfactory for a sample inspection. While standard electronics factories use AQL 2.5 for major defects (such as power button unresponsiveness) and AQL 4.0 for minor cosmetic flaws, BOHOL applies tightened AQL 0.65 inspection. In a batch of 10,000 units, AQL 0.65 requires inspecting 315 units and rejects the entire lot if more than 5 non-conformities are found.
