Twist & Fun and Starship take different approaches to a male wellness device. The most useful comparison starts with their construction, movement and controls—not simply the number of settings on a product sheet.

## Two different constructions

[Twist & Fun](/products/twist-fun/) uses a cylindrical body with a transparent viewing window. Its LCD panel and exterior buttons sit above that window. The listed dimensions are 11.81 × 3.70 × 3.70 inches, with a weight of 900 g.

[Starship](/products/starship/) has a compact, open-form body. Its cutaway exterior exposes the inner construction, and the product sheet lists dimensions of 6.94 × 3.65 × 2.55 inches and a net weight of 363 g.

These measurements describe the whole device. They should not be interpreted as internal dimensions or a promise of fit.

## Compare the movement itself

Twist & Fun combines twisting and telescopic movement. Starship lists nine rotation settings and nine vibration settings. Those descriptions refer to different mechanisms and controls; they are not directly interchangeable measures of performance.

Look at the full product photographs and read the operating instructions for the model you are considering. A mode count alone cannot describe the complete experience of a device.

## Charging is model-specific

The Twist & Fun sheet lists USB-C charging and a three-hour charging time. Starship lists a DC socket and a 75-minute charging time. Use the accessories and power requirements specified for the exact model.

## A practical way to choose

- Start with the exterior shape and the available storage space.
- Compare overall dimensions and weight.
- Check the movement type and how the controls are arranged.
- Confirm charging requirements and included accessories with your seller.

The specifications in this guide are drawn from the supplied product sheets. Production variants and included accessories should be confirmed against the exact model being purchased.
