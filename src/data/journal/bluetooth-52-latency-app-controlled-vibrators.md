## How Does Human Tissue Absorption Affect Bluetooth RF Signal Propagation?

The human body is composed of approximately 60% water, which acts as a powerful absorber of 2.4 GHz radio frequencies. When an internal or wearable vibrator is positioned against the body or under heavy bedcovers, RF signal attenuation can exceed -25 dB, causing traditional PCB trace antennas to disconnect. BOHOL hardware engineers decouple the ceramic patch antenna from the internal battery ground plane, positioning it directly beneath the thinnest exterior silicone boundary to maintain a robust RF link up to 15 meters.

## What Cybersecurity Protocols Prevent Unauthorized Device Hijacking?

IoT intimate devices face severe privacy concerns if Bluetooth pairing is left unauthenticated. BOHOL firmware implements BLE Secure Connections using Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman (ECDH) P-256 key exchange. A physical confirmation button on the device must be pressed to pair a new smartphone, completely preventing unauthorized nearby strangers from detecting or controlling the toy.
