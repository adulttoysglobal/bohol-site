## What Is Hansen Solubility Parameter and Why Does Siloxane Swelling Occur?

The chemical phenomenon of material degradation is governed by the Hansen Solubility Parameter (HSP), which evaluates dispersion, polar, and hydrogen bonding energy between substances. Because silicone lubricants (dimethicone) and cured liquid silicone rubber have nearly identical HSP values (approx. 15.5 MPa½), silicone lubricant acts as a low-energy solvent. The liquid siloxane molecules migrate between the cross-linked silicone chains, causing the polymer to swell, soften, and dissolve its smooth outer skin.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use silicone lubricant with toys made of hard ABS plastic, glass, or stainless steel?
Yes. Silicone lubricant is completely safe to use with hard, non-porous materials like ABS plastic, borosilicate glass, and 304 stainless steel, as these materials do not contain siloxane bonds.

### What should a customer do if they accidentally put silicone lubricant on a silicone toy?
Immediately wash the toy with grease-cutting dish soap and warm water to remove residual oil. If washed off within a few minutes, permanent surface swelling may be minimized.
