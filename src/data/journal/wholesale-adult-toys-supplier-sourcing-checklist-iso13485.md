## 1. Auditing Factory Cleanrooms: Why Medical-Grade ISO 13485 Matters

When sourcing intimate wellness products, cosmetic appearance can be deceptive. A low-tier manufacturing workshop using recycled thermoplastic elastomer (TPE) and mineral oil plasticizers might present a visually appealing prototype. However, microscopic pores and residual chemical monomers rapidly trigger epidermal irritation, bacterial colonization, and catastrophic brand recall liabilities.

True Tier-1 manufacturing mandates ISO 13485:2016 certified medical device quality management systems operating inside positive-pressure Class 100,000 cleanrooms. In these controlled microenvironments, airborne particulates are strictly limited to fewer than 3,520,000 particles per cubic meter (≥0.5 µm). All liquid silicone rubber (LSR) injection molding occurs inside fully closed-loop robotic dosing cells, ensuring zero human skin contact and eliminating airborne particulate contamination during high-temperature vulcanization.
