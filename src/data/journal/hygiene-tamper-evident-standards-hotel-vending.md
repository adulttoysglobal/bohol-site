## 1. Multi-Layer Tamper-Evident Packaging

Guests must have absolute confidence that no previous room occupant has handled or tampered with their purchase. Every BOHOL product dispensed in hotel kiosks is protected by three layers of hygiene defense: 1. An outer 25-micron POF clear shrink-wrap barrier; 2. A holographic VOID security tear sticker across the box flap that reveals 'OPENED' if lifted; 3. Hermetically sealed inner aluminum blister packs.

## 2. Automated Cloud Expiration Lockout

Human staff can occasionally forget to check printed expiration dates on boxes inside vending slots. BOHOL’s intelligent firmware solves this automatically. When stocking new inventory, housekeeping scans the box QR code, recording its manufacturing lot and expiration date into the cloud. If an item reaches 90 days prior to expiry, the machine automatically disables that slot and sends a notification to replace the unit.
