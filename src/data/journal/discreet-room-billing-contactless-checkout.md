## 1. Decoupling Retail from the Front Desk Room Folio

In legacy hotel minibars, snacks and items are posted to the room bill. When a corporate traveler checks out, their company finance department reviews every itemized line. BOHOL smart kiosks operate as an independent direct-to-consumer micro-terminal. Payment is completed instantly on the guest’s personal smartphone via Apple Pay, Google Pay, or private credit card. Nothing is ever added to the hotel room folio or corporate invoice.
