## What Words Trigger Instant Automated Suspensions on Major E-Commerce Platforms?

Automated compliance filters trigger warnings when product copy or packaging includes therapeutic claims such as 'cures erectile dysfunction', 'treats vaginismus', 'pelvic organ prolapse therapy', or 'medical pelvic rehab'. Under US FDA and EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR), claiming physiological healing transforms a novelty device into an unapproved Class II medical device. BOHOL's regulatory team drafts compliant marketing copy focusing strictly on sensory exploration, ergonomic pleasure, and novelty relaxation.

## How Does Turnkey Retail Packaging Solve Payment Processor (Stripe/Shopify) Underwriting Audits?

When merchant payment processors conduct periodic spot audits, they demand full photographs of the physical retail box, batch serial numbers, factory ISO certifications, and laboratory RoHS test reports. If the packaging looks like a generic generic white box without manufacturer traceability, merchant accounts are frozen immediately.
