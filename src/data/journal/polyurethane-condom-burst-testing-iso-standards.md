## What Is the Automated Air Burst Testing Protocol?

In the metrology testing bay, a sample condom is clamped over a calibrated pneumatic air nozzle inside an enclosed clear acrylic blast cabinet. Compressed air is pumped into the condom at a constant flow rate of 0.4 to 0.5 liters per second. High-precision digital pressure transducers and optical volumetric sensors record the exact burst pressure (in kilopascals) and total air volume (in liters) at the millisecond of rupture, certifying that the polymer can withstand extreme real-world friction and pressure spikes.

## How Does 100% Electronic Water Bath Pinhole Testing Guarantee Zero Leaks?

While air burst testing destroys samples statistically, pinhole testing inspects every single condom intended for packaging. Each condom is mounted onto a stainless steel electrical mandrel and submerged in an electrolyte bath. A high-voltage DC test current is applied. Because polyurethane is a non-conductive electrical insulator, zero current passes through a flawless condom. If even a 1-micron pinhole exists, ions conduct electrical current instantly, triggering an automated robotic arm to reject the defective unit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can sharp fingernails puncture a 0.01mm polyurethane condom while opening the foil?
While polyurethane has very high tensile strength, all thin membranes are vulnerable to sharp shear forces. BOHOL uses easy-tear 'buttercup' and V-notch foil packaging designed to open with gentle finger pressure without touching the condom.

### How often are air burst machines calibrated?
Our digital flow meters and pressure transducers undergo weekly calibration against traceable national metrology laboratory standards to ensure ±0.5% measurement accuracy.
