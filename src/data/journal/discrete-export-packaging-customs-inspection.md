## What Exterior Packaging Elements Trigger Customs Scrutiny?

Customs officers scanning air cargo manifests or inspecting seaport container doors are trained to flag packages with ambiguous, controversial, or provocative branding. If outer master cartons feature bright colors, seductive female silhouettes, or bold words like 'Adult Toys' or 'Pleasure', inspectors are obligated by port operating procedures to open the cartons for full physical examination. This adds 7 to 14 days of port demurrage fees and increases cargo damage risk.

## What Are the Specifications of Industrial-Grade 5-Ply Neutral Master Cartons?

BOHOL manufactures export master cartons using five-ply double-wall corrugated paperboard (BC-Flute, 250# burst strength). The exterior is plain unprinted natural kraft brown with a smooth moisture-resistant waterborne coating. Box flaps are sealed with reinforced fiberglass water-activated gummed tape. The only markings on the exterior carton are discrete black-ink shipping marks indicating carton number (e.g. 'Carton 14 of 120'), gross weight, net weight, dimensions, and standard international handling icons (Fragile, Keep Dry, This Way Up).
