## 1. The Hidden Dangers of Cheap 'Jelly Rubber' and PVC

Many budget novelty items sold at gas stations or unverified marketplaces are molded from PVC plastic softened with chemical plasticizers like DEHP and DBP. These compounds emit a strong petrochemical odor, degrade into a sticky residue over time, and are classified as endocrine disruptors that interfere with reproductive hormones. Furthermore, jelly rubber is microscopically porous, trapping bacterial colonies even after thorough washing.

## 2. Platinum-Cured LSR: The Gold Standard of Biocompatibility

Liquid Silicone Rubber (LSR) undergoes a platinum-catalyzed addition reaction that creates a stable three-dimensional siloxane polymer chain. There are zero volatile compounds to leach out. It can withstand temperatures up to 200°C without melting, meaning you can sanitize the silicone surface with boiling water without fear of chemical decomposition.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How can I tell if an adult toy is real silicone or fake jelly rubber?
Real silicone feels silky, velvety, and completely dry to the touch with zero chemical smell. Fake jelly rubber feels greasy, sticky, smells like strong solvent or plastic shower curtains, and attracts lint instantly.

### Can silicone toys be boiled for sterilization?
Pure silicone components (like sleeves or Kegel balls without motors) can be boiled in water for 3 minutes. For battery-powered vibrators, wash thoroughly with antibacterial toy cleanser instead.
