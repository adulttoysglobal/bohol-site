## 1. Carry-On vs. Checked Baggage: Lithium-Ion Safety

International aviation authorities (FAA, EASA, ICAO) strictly prohibit loose lithium-ion batteries in checked cargo holds due to fire hazard risks. Because all modern vibrators (like [Bite](/products/bite/) and [Xiaoqiao](/products/xiaoqiao/)) contain rechargeable lithium cells, they **must be carried in your cabin baggage**. If checked luggage security spots a lithium battery on X-ray, they will open your bag and may confiscate the item.

## 2. How Travel Lock Functions Prevent Embarrassment

When luggage shifts in overhead bins, clothing can press against the power button of a toy, turning on a high-speed vibration that causes the entire bin to hum. Premium devices like [Bite](/products/bite/) feature an intelligent Travel Lock: holding down the power button for 3 to 5 seconds locks the circuit board. The toy will not turn on until the unlock sequence is repeated.

### Are adult toys legal in all international travel destinations?
