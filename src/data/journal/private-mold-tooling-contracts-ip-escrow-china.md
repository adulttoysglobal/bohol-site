For independent brand owners, Kickstarter innovators, and enterprise distributors entering the intimate hardware market, proprietary industrial design is the primary competitive moat. Commissioning custom injection tooling (steel molds) in China requires a transparent, enforceable contractual framework guaranteeing 100% exclusive mold ownership, non-disclosure protection, and strict anti-circumvention covenants.

## 1. The Legal Architecture: NNN Agreements vs Standard NDAs

A standard Western Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is largely ineffective in Chinese jurisprudence because it only covers public disclosure. At BOHOL, we execute bilingual **NNN Agreements (Non-Disclosure, Non-Use, Non-Circumvention)** enforceable under Chinese contract law:

- **Non-Use:** Guarantees that the factory will never utilize the client’s CAD models, PCB schematics, or packaging artwork for any other customer or internal house brand.
- **Non-Circumvention:** Prohibits the factory from selling finished goods manufactured from the tooling directly to the client’s distributors, Amazon sellers, or retail partners.
- **Enforceability in Local Jurisdiction:** Written in Chinese and English, governed by Chinese civil courts with jurisdiction in Guangdong, ensuring immediate injunctive relief if breached.

## 2. Engineering Standards: Steel Selection & Maintenance

The longevity of custom injection molds directly dictates unit economics over multi-year production runs. BOHOL uses only genuine imported steel grades:

1. **Swedish ASSAB / Stavax S136 Stainless Steel:** Hardened to 50–54 HRC (Rockwell Hardness). Impervious to the corrosive off-gassing of liquid silicone rubber and fluorosilicones, guaranteeing over 500,000 continuous injection shots without dimensional drift.
2. **5-Axis High-Speed CNC Milling:** Machined on German DMG MORI 5-axis machining centers with mirror-spark EDM (Electrical Discharge Machining) down to Ra 0.05 surface smoothness.
3. **Routine Maintenance Protocol:** Every 20,000 shots, molds undergo ultrasonic degreasing, runner re-polishing, and complete replacement of silicone O-ring hermetic seals at zero maintenance charge to the client.
