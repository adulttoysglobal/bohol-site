## 1. Nerve Mapping: Coronal Ridge and Frenulum Stimulation

The highest density of sensory nerve endings on the male anatomy is concentrated around the frenulum (the underside of the head) and the coronal ridge. In designs like the [Big Mouth](/products/big-mouth/) and [NR-MAST-1](/products/nr-mast-1/), the entrance vestibule is lined with dense horizontal silicone ridges. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do some textures feel too intense for beginners?
Dense raised nodules can cause sensory overload if you are unaccustomed to textured stimulation. Beginners should use extra water-based lubricant to soften friction, or choose sleeves with wavy gentle ribbing before trying aggressive spiked patterns.

### How much lubricant should I put inside a textured sleeve?
Pour approximately 10 to 15 ml of lubricant into the entrance and coat your shaft thoroughly. Insufficient lubrication prevents the textures from rolling smoothly and can cause skin irritation.
