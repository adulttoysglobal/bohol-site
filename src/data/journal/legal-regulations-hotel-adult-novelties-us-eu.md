## How Do Digital Payment Terminals Satisfy Statutory 18+ Age Verification Requirements?

Under US COPPA, state obscenity laws, and the UK/EU Online Safety Directives, distributing adult materials requires reasonable verification of majority age. Because BOHOL kiosks require payment via an active credit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay—all of which require the account holder to possess a verified bank account and legal age of majority—the payment transaction itself acts as an audited, legally recognized age verification checkpoint.

## What Electrical Safety Standards Must In-Room Terminals Satisfy to Satisfy Hotel Fire Insurance?

Hotel commercial insurance policies require that any permanently plugged or hardwired electrical device hold verified NRTL (Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory) or CE electrical safety certification. BOHOL terminals utilize low-voltage 12V DC internal step-down transformers, UL-certified external power supplies, flame-retardant internal wiring (UL94-V0), and over-current surge suppression, ensuring zero risk of electrical fire.
