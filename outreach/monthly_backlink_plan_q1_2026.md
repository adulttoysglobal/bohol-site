### A. The 4 Mandatory Screening Criteria (四个指标组合拳)
Every candidate backlink domain must pass all four gates simultaneously:
1. **Industry Relevance (行业相关性)**: Adult tech, personal wellness, medical liquid silicone (LSR), injection molding, CNC tooling, hotel vending IoT, or B2B sourcing/trade.
2. **Real Organic Traffic (真实自然流量)**: Verified organic Google search traffic (>1,000 monthly visits). Zero tolerance for link farms or PBNs.
3. **Authority Metrics (权重指标)**: Domain Authority (DA) ≥ 25, Page Authority (PA) ≥ 20. Primary focus on DA 50–95.
4. **Spam Score (垃圾评分)**: Spam Score (SS) ≤ 5%. Low risk of algorithmic penalty (Penguin safe).

### B. Golden Anchor Text Allocation (5类锚文本黄金比例)
Avoid over-optimizing for commercial keywords. Maintain this exact ratio:
- **Brand Keywords (品牌词) [45%]**: `BOHOL`, `BOHOL Adult Toys`, `BOHOL Smart Manufacturing`, `BOHOL Factory`
- **Naked / Direct URL (直接网址) [30%]**: `https://adulttoysglobal.com/`, `adulttoysglobal.com`, `https://adulttoysglobal.com/tools/customs-hs-code-calculator/`
- **Generic Descriptors (普通描述词) [10%]**: `learn more`, `source`, `view full technical specifications`, `visit official website`
- **Target Keywords (精准关键词) [5%]**: `adult toy manufacturer`, `smart hotel vending machine OEM`, `silicone vibrator factory`
- **Natural LSI / Long-Tail Variants (其他自然变体) [10%]**: `medical-grade liquid silicone manufacturing at BOHOL`, `custom silicone personal massagers engineered by BOHOL`
